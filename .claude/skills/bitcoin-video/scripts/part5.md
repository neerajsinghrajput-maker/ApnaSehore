## Story beats

- Viewers picked the Corrupt CA. The CA's masterplan: a shell company — a fake IT company that
  exists only on paper, opened on a poor villager's documents ("Tech Solutions Pvt Ltd").
- Round trip: your crypto → Dubai → fake "software consulting" payment → your own company.
  Government sees dollars coming in (software export, low tax).
- ₹1 crore landed in the company's current account; you got a corporate credit card.
- Income Tax / GST AI flagged it: ₹1 crore turnover, but no office rent, no electricity bill,
  no employee PF. Officers found a shut chai stall at the address.
- CA called: raid, the ₹1 crore corporate account is seized.
- While running, you spotted a hidden laptop folder: "PROJECT BLACK".
- Choice: Goa casino, or open the secret folder? → Part 6.
