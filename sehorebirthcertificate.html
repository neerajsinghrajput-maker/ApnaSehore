Sehore Me Birth Certificate Kaise Banwaye

Agar aap Sehore me birth certificate banana chahte hain, to yeh guide aapko online aur offline dono process step-by-step samjhayegi.

Birth Certificate Kya Hota Hai?

Birth certificate ek official document hai jo janam ki date, jagah aur parents ki details confirm karta hai.

Required Documents

  • Hospital discharge slip
  • Parents Aadhar card
  • Address proof
  • Marriage certificate (optional)

Online Process (Sehore)

  1. MP e-Nagar Palika portal visit kare
  2. Birth registration option select kare
  3. Form fill karke documents upload kare
  4. Application submit kare

Offline Process

Sehore Nagar Palika office jaakar aap birth certificate ke liye apply kar sakte hain.

Office Address:
Nagar Palika Sehore, Madhya Pradesh

Fees & Time

Normal fees ₹20–₹50 hoti hai aur certificate 7–15 din me mil jata hai.

Note: Information time ke sath change ho sakti hai.
