# Master prompt — Bitcoin series 3x3 comic grid

Use with Gemini or ChatGPT image generation. Fill the 9 `PANEL` lines from the part's script beats
(in order); keep everything else exactly as written so every part looks the same.
Best results: attach the previous part's grid image and add "Match the exact art style, character
design and colours of the attached image."

```
Create ONE single SQUARE image (1:1, highest resolution) that is a comic storyboard grid of
9 equal square panels in 3 columns x 3 rows, read left-to-right, top-to-bottom.
Thin pure-white gutters (about 20px) between panels and a clean black border (about 8px) around
every panel. Nothing outside the panels.

ART STYLE (same in all 9 panels): modern Indian webcomic, bold clean black ink outlines, flat cel
shading with soft gradients, cinematic night mood lighting in purple, deep blue and neon pink,
warm lamp glow for indoor scenes, slightly gritty textures, high detail, sharp and crisp.

MAIN CHARACTER (identical in every panel he appears in): a young Indian man whose head is a
perfectly smooth plain WHITE ROUND BALL (no hair, no nose, no ears), big simple cartoon eyes and
eyebrows that show emotion, black hands, blue-and-white checked shirt with sleeves rolled up,
dark blue jeans, slim build.
Other people (dealers, police, mafia, officers) are drawn as realistic Indian comic characters.

COMPOSITION RULES (the panels will be cropped to vertical 9:16 for a reel):
- keep the main subject and any important object inside the CENTRAL 55% of each panel's width;
- leave the bottom 15% of each panel free of faces and important text;
- one clear action per panel, strong silhouette, close or medium shots, no tiny details.

TEXT RULES: no captions, no speech bubbles, no narration boxes. Only short on-screen UI text
where the panel says so (phone/laptop screens, signs, stamps): IN ENGLISH, CAPITAL LETTERS,
max 4 words, large, bold, correctly spelled, fully inside the panel. Rupee amounts like
"₹4,94,00,000" written exactly. No other text, logos or watermarks.

PANEL 1: <hook moment of this part>
PANEL 2: <...>
PANEL 3: <...>
PANEL 4: <...>
PANEL 5: <...>
PANEL 6: <...>
PANEL 7: <...>
PANEL 8: <...>
PANEL 9: split screen of the two choices for the next part, left half <option A> with a black
         label "OPTION A", right half <option B> with a black label "OPTION B", shown on a monitor.
```

## Writing the 9 panel lines
- Panel 1 = the first-3-second hook; panel 9 = always the two-option cliffhanger (labels = the
  exact comment options).
- Spread the script's beats over panels 2–8: setup → plan → how it works → the catch / twist →
  consequence → the danger closing in.
- Describe: who, doing what, the emotion on the white ball face, the key object, the light colour.
  Put numbers/status words on screens, e.g. `laptop screen shows "SEND ₹50,00,000"`.
- Vary shots: wide room, close-up of face, close-up of a screen, over-the-shoulder, outdoor night.

## If one panel comes out wrong
Regenerate only that panel (then send it as its own image):
"Single square comic panel, <same ART STYLE + MAIN CHARACTER lines>, <the panel line>,
subject in the central 55% of the width, no captions."
